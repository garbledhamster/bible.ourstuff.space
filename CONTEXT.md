# Project Bible

## Language

**Project Bible**

The project's record of its scope, accepted decisions, and evidence, organized by chapter.

**Wayfinder map**

An index for an effort's destination, open questions, dependencies, and resolved decision tickets.

**Decision ticket**

An answerable question whose resolution guides project work. A closed ticket links to the accepted decision in the Project Bible.

**Build ticket**

An agreed piece of work with a deliverable and completion checks.
