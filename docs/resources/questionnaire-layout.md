# Project Bible questionnaire layout

Use this layout when a contributor needs to read a shared project update before answering a round. The example uses a fictional project and contributors. Follow [Contributor Training](game-bible-guide.md#coordinate-contributors-in-rounds) for what to share, where to save each round, and how to carry decisions forward.

## Put the round picker above the thread

Lead with the recipient’s name. Keep the project name nearby, then show the current round number and title in a compact picker with the estimated time beside it or just below it. Opening the picker reveals the rounds the recipient can access. Mark the selected round, and update the round content and action together when they choose another one. Let contributors revisit their own earlier answers through the authorized history.

The built-in **Grilling** session picker lists sessions across the Bible. A crew questionnaire’s picker lists rounds within that crew questionnaire. Use round numbers and names on the recipient form so contributors can tell which set of questions they’re opening.

## Put shared context in a conversation

Use a clear heading such as **Shared project update** and a sentence that says why the contributor is seeing it. Match the Project Bible 2.0 reader. Use its dark masthead, lavender chapter titles, pale paper, dark ink, and thin lavender rules. Keep Instrument Serif for display titles and DM Sans for controls and form text. Use deep purple for the primary action and gold to mark the selected round.

| Whose update | Placement | What the reader sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipient | Left, pale sage | Their name, **Your Round 1 summary**, and a short recap. |
| Teammate | Right, pale lavender | Their name, the round, and a recap of relevant work or answers. |
| Coordinator | Right, pale amber | Their name, **Coordinator update**, and what they’re working on for this round. |

Give each item a readable title and preview. Clicking or tapping its header expands the detail inline, so the reader keeps their place. Use a focusable disclosure control that exposes its open or closed state. Keep names and roles visible; the colors help readers scan.

- **Collapse all** closes the expanded detail and leaves the titles and previews visible.
- **Hide all** removes the summary cards from view and leaves the context heading and **Show summaries** control. **Show summaries** brings the cards back.

## Keep the primary action in reach

Place **Start questionnaire** in a bottom action row. Arrange the intro so the context can scroll above that row and the action never covers a summary or its focused control. Allow room for the phone’s safe area. Once a contributor starts, give the questions and choices the most room. Keep the short **Why we’re asking** note beneath each question as described in the [questionnaire style guide](game-bible-guide.md#style-project-bible-questionnaires).

Run the real round at 100% zoom on desktop and on a short phone. Open the picker, select a round, expand a long summary, use both bulk controls, and start the round by keyboard and touch. Check wrapping, focus, and increased zoom. When saving the reference, [keep the full-resolution artwork](concept-boards.md#keep-full-resolution-artwork) and compare desktop and phone views side by side.
