## What to build

Use the [Audio Engineering chapter](../game-bible-guide.md#audio-engineering) to plan the release’s music, make the effects, and record any dialogue for one representative encounter. Wire the exports into the game and listen to the mix during play.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] The release records where and why to use music or silence. For each track, define its style, placement, playback and transitions, level beside cues and dialogue, source, and rights or credits.
- [ ] Each cue has a game event, priority, playback and stop behavior, a build file, and source and rights information.
- [ ] For recorded dialogue, link the line sheet, selected takes, captions or prompts, and permission to use the recordings.
- [ ] The encounter has a resettable case and can be completed with sound off.
- [ ] On target devices, the important cues are clear in the mix and play once at the intended moments. Try mute, rapid input, pause and resume, and exit.
- [ ] For a silent release, record the decision and verify the other feedback.

## Evidence and handoff

Link the music plan, cue list, and listening record with the build, devices, and observed results. Name the remaining checks that carry into the Sandbox.
