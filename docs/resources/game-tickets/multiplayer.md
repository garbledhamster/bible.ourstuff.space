## What to build

Follow [Multiplayer & Multi-user Support](../game-bible-guide.md#multiplayer--multi-user-support) and the [hosting walkthrough](../multiplayer-hosting.md). Deliver the modes in scope, or record N/A and the single-player decision.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] Scope names mode, player count, devices, shared activity, and persistence.
- [ ] Independent players can join, finish an encounter, leave, reconnect where supported, and restart.
- [ ] Shared outcomes pass simultaneous and invalid-action checks. Server authority and identity are documented for online games.
- [ ] Real devices pass the local and remote joining paths promised; latency, disconnect, and server restart are covered.
- [ ] Hosting has a known owner, pinned release, budget, setup and operating runbook. Cloud and persistent-world claims have deployment, load, and restore evidence as applicable.
- [ ] Demo, help, release claims, and contributor setup match the actual modes.

## Evidence and handoff

Record client/server revisions, targets, network conditions, expected and observed outcomes, evidence links, and remaining checks. A plan or two open browser tabs does not establish remote play or MMO readiness.
