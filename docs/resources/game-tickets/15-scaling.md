## What to build

Use the [Scaling chapter](../game-bible-guide.md#scaling) and its [trial record](../game-bible-template.md#scaling) to test one increment of a working release or process. If the evidence gives you no reason to grow now, record the decision and what would reopen it; the trial checks can wait.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] State the outcome, the unit, today's size, and the demand or adoption evidence for the next size.
- [ ] Measure the baseline and identify what limits good results. Choose one change and set trial conditions, an owner, and quality and cost or workload limits.
- [ ] Run a bounded trial and rehearse stopping and recovering affected work. Compare the result against the baseline and agreed limits.
- [ ] Record whether to expand, hold, or back out, and who owns the next check. If there is no current reason to grow, record why and what would reopen the decision.

## Evidence and handoff

Link the trial record, measured results, and recovery evidence. Record the size and conditions actually tested. If the promise changes, bring the evidence to Vision & Scope.
